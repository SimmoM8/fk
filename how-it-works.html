How FK Works

FK helps you find your perfect condom size through a simple 4-step process built around girth, comfort, and confidence.

Step-by-step guidance

From first check to confident order

Follow each step in order. Every stage is designed to help you verify fit and buy with certainty.

  1. 1

    Find your condom size

    Choose how you want to begin. If you already know your girth, FK can recommend a size immediately.

    I know my girth I don't know my girth

    If you know your girth

    Measure your girth in inches, remove the decimal, and use that two-digit number as your FK size. Example: 4.5 becomes 45.

    If you don't know your girth

    You can still continue with the testing kit. Measuring first gives the most accurate recommendation.

  2. 2

    Order your FK testing kit

    The testing kit helps you confirm your most comfortable, secure size before committing to full packs.

    If you know your size

    Your kit includes your recommended size, two sizes smaller, and two sizes larger, plus custom measuring tape and FK water-based lube.

    Example: if your size is 37, your kit includes 33, 35, 37, 39, 41.

    If you don't know your size

    • 5 condoms covering a typical size range
    • Custom measuring tape
    • FK water-based lube
    • Convenient storage design

  3. 3

    Test the fit

    Keep the process simple and consistent each time you test.

    1. Get erect
    2. Put on the condom
    3. Check the fit

    Quick fit guidance

    • If it feels too tight: go up a size
    • If it feels too loose: go down a size
    • If it feels snug and secure: that's your size

    Quick check

    Grip halfway up the penis and gently pull. If it resists but does not slip off, the size is correct.
